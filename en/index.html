Renato Augusto TavaresComputer scientist. Goiânia, Brazil.

I work on problems in number theory where the computer is part of the argument, and I write the software that makes that argument checkable.

My current research is on the 3n+1 problem and Wirsching’s program for positive predecessor density. Research

  • Wirsching’s program for positive predecessor density

    In 2003, Günther Wirsching reduced uniform positive predecessor density for the 3n+1 map to a chain of five conditions organized into three conjectures. A 2026 preprint proves Conjectures 1 and 3 and reduces the chain to a single condition, (⋆3). Conjecture 2, and positive predecessor density itself, remain open.

    • Conjectures 1 and 3 (preprint)
    • Conjecture 2, positive density

    Notes in Portuguese

  • Wirsching’s Weak Covering Conjecture

    Exact computation of the covering budget j*(ℓ) extended from ℓ = 20 to ℓ = 23; an unconditional proof that the excess over the counting rate is unbounded; a linear upper bound conditional on a computationally verified correspondence with a mean-payoff game; and theorems on the residues that resist coverage. The conjecture remains open.

    • Exact table to ℓ = 23
    • e(ℓ) ≥ ¼ log₂ ℓ − O(1)
    • j*(ℓ) ≤ 9ℓ/8 + 33/2
    • The conjecture

    Notes in Portuguese

  • The counting exponent of the 5x+1 reverse tree

    A calibrated computational study comparing the Kontorovich–Lagarias prediction (0.650919) with Volkov’s (0.678). Under stated, unproved assumptions, the calibrated reading of the arithmetic tree is consistent with the former. This is computational evidence, not a proof.

    • Calibrated reading: 0.64926
    • Existence of the exponent

  • Image processing and computer vision

    The subject of my master’s studies. A 2024 preprint evaluates image preprocessing techniques for licence-plate OCR on Brazilian plates and reports a negative result: none of the techniques outperformed the baseline.

    • Experimental study

Markers show the status of each result: proved in a preprint, conditional, computational evidence, conjecture, open. None of this work has been peer reviewed yet.

I try to keep theorems, conditional results, empirical observations and open questions explicitly apart; to use exact arithmetic or certified interval arithmetic whenever a number enters a statement; to write independent implementations of expensive computations; and to archive code and data with a DOI. I also use multi-agent AI systems as research instruments, for literature search, hypothesis generation, code auditing and manuscript critique. Direction, claims and responsibility remain mine.
